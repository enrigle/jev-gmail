# Terms of Service — jev-gmail

jev-gmail is a personal, non-commercial script that sorts its owner's Gmail inbox into labels.
It is not offered as a service to anyone else.

- **Use**: only the owner's own Gmail account, with access the owner grants and can revoke
  at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.
- **No warranty**: provided "as is". Labels are automatic guesses and can be wrong;
  the script never deletes or sends email.
- **Third parties**: email text is processed by OpenRouter and TypeSafe under their own terms.
